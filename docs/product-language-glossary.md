# Product language glossary

WEPACKER product and domain names are canonical English names. They remain
unchanged in `pt-PT` and `en-US` UI:

- My Journey
- Basecamp
- Life Map
- Goals
- Trails
- Actions
- Sessions
- Mentorships
- Connections
- Packs
- Academy

Use the canonical spelling and capitalization when the text names the product
concept, including a singular item such as a Goal, Trail, Action, Session,
Mentorship, Connection, or Pack. Translate the surrounding sentence.

Generic interface language is localized. For example, Portuguese uses
`Perfil`, `Notificações`, `Comunidades`, `Pessoas`, `Ver`, `Editar`, `Eliminar`,
`Filtros`, and localized status labels; English uses their English equivalents.
Generic words remain translatable even when they appear beside a product name,
for example `Próxima Session` and `Trails ativos`.

Stages (`Easy Peasy`, `Step Up`, `YUP`) and the Six Pillars (`Physical`,
`Emotional`, `Character`, `Spiritual`, `Intellectual`, `Social`) also retain
their canonical English names, as defined in `CLAUDE.md`.
